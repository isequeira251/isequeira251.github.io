From a portal nobody trusted to a working revenue engine.

  • Started read-only: a four-report portal audit (data hygiene, pipeline, renewals coverage, workflow cruft) that drove every build decision after it.
  • Territory and market-tier models operationalized from the client's own strategy spreadsheet and backfilled across roughly 10,000 records — nearly 3,000 API writes, zero failures — lifting owner coverage from near-zero to about a quarter of the database in one pass.
  • Weekly lead-score history snapshots and trend dashboards — score history HubSpot itself doesn't keep.
  • Note-to-meeting conversion and association-based activity logic that corrected a ~5× undercount in held meetings.
  • Daily leadership digest email and a monthly exec sales workbook that regenerates itself from a read-only pull.
  • A weekly AI competitive-intelligence agent tracking 10 competitors and delivering sourced briefs with counter-marketing angles — zero recurring manual effort.

Industrial-compliance SaaS · ongoing

Tens of thousands of contacts organized, scored, and explained — in days.

A large existing database with no structure on top: no buying-committee roles, no account scoring, and no record of why deals were being lost. The brief was explicit — organize what we already own.

  • AI stakeholder-role enrichment: tens of thousands of titled contacts classified into a six-role buying-committee taxonomy within 48 hours, for roughly $20 of API cost — with a confidence gate that wrote only high-confidence labels and routed the rest to human review instead of polluting the CRM.
  • A two-tier design so new contacts get labeled in real time by mined keyword rules, and the AI only handles what keywords can't.
  • Fit-based A–D account scoring written to thousands of companies, refreshed weekly with historical snapshots.
  • Conversion analysis across hundreds of deals that found the real win levers: multi-threaded deals won at roughly 2.5× the rate of single-threaded ones, and deals with the economic buyer in the room won markedly more often.
  • Loss reasons recovered for over 90% of closed-lost deals from activity timelines — the loss-reason field was blank on every single one.
  • A weekday-morning digest so sales leadership sees portal activity without logging in.

How I work

Your CRM is production. I treat it that way.

The real fear in hiring a consultant isn't the invoice — it's someone breaking the system your revenue runs through. Every engagement runs on the same safety rails:

Read-only first

Audits never write. You get findings and a plan before a single property is created or a single record is touched.

Dry-run by default

Every write script reports exactly what it would change before it changes anything. Bulk operations ship with the receipts.

Canary-guarded rollouts

New workflows are built disabled, validated end-to-end against guarded test records in the live portal, and only enabled with your sign-off.

Monitored, not fire-and-forget

A daily health monitor checks every scheduled job I run and reports pass/fail/overdue each morning. Failures surface in hours — not when you notice a report went stale.
